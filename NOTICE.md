# Third-party and prior-work notices

The proof uses the following previously established mathematics and software.

- Baumgartner and Hajnal's nested-interval partition constructions and the
  related work of Chen, Garti, and Weinert.
- The Nešetřil–Rödl ordering property and sparse-hypergraph method.
- Baumgartner's positive partition theorem under Martin's axiom.
- Gödel's and Solovay–Tennenbaum's relative-consistency results.
- Shelah's nonuniversality theory and Li's related ordinal-graph approach.
- Mathlib and Lean, at the exact revisions in the project lockfiles.
- Flypitch, originally by Jesse Michael Han and Floris van Doorn. The local
  Lean 4 compatibility port derives from the public port credited to Ian
  Klatzco and Claude. This repository pins the actual included files by content,
  and does not claim that the upstream default branch matches those files.
- Jan Hubička's relational and Ramsey formalization in partite-construction
  and its pinned successor-tree dependency.
- The original graph-target statement adapted from Formal Conjectures,
  revision dc3e07b902f6b2df333e2f90674c73232108d5dc, under Apache License 2.0.
