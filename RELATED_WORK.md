# Prior work and priority scope

The manuscript credits Baumgartner–Hajnal and Chen–Garti–Weinert for the
nested-interval method, Nešetřil–Rödl for the ordering-property method, and
Baumgartner for the positive Martin-axiom theorem. These classical ingredients
are retained in the bibliography as prior results.

The full Baumgartner 1989 chapter was inspected. Theorem 3.5(a), printed
pages 14–15, supplies the stated positive partition relation for every finite
clique size. The full Nešetřil–Rödl 1978 paper was also inspected.

[Li's 2026 preprint](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22671917) supplies a related
nonuniversality approach to the unrestricted question. The present manuscript
uses an explicit nested graph and also proves the finite-target independence
statement. Shelah's earlier nonuniversality work is credited separately.

The literature and public-repository review found no earlier exact finite-target
independence proof in the inspected material. First priority remains unverified.
Full-text gaps remain for the 1987 Baumgartner–Hajnal chapter and the 2025
Komjáth survey. An independently corroborated public proof-completion date
remains pending release.

Public PRs 3133 and 2331 in the awards repository are closed and unmerged.
Their submitted mapping of JSP-000484 to Erdős 484 differs from the historical
ordinal-partition catalog entry. The submission packet records this discrepancy
and supplies the original Erdős 597 statement for review.
